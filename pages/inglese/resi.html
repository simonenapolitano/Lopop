30-day returns

Changed your mind, or is the item not what you expected? Lopop allows returns within 30 days.

Return Period

You can request a return within 30 days of receiving your order. Keep your purchase confirmation and check the applicable terms before proceeding.

When are returns free?

The site advertises free returns on orders over €29.90. Check your order summary and terms to make sure you meet the threshold.

Preparing Your Item

Before returning a product, make sure it is complete and properly protected for transport. Use the original packaging where possible, and keep any tags and accessories received with the item.

To start a return request, email returns@lopop.it with your order number, reason for the return, and any supporting images. Our team will get back to you in less than 2 days!
