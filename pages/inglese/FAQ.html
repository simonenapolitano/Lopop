FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about shopping, payments, shipping and returns. For more information, see our page about support, alle deliveries e ai returns.

Accounts and Shopping

How can I create an account?

Select the profile icon in the header and choose “Sign up”. Fill in the required fields to create your account.

Do I need to log in to use the cart?

Yes. You need to log in to view your cart. If you do not have an account yet, you can create one on the registration page.

Which payment methods can I use?

Available payment methods are shown during checkout, before you confirm your order.

Shipping and Delivery

How much does shipping cost?

The site offers standard delivery. Any applicable charges are shown at checkout before you confirm your purchase. For more information, visit the page Shipping and Delivery.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Check delivery times during checkout. The site currently does not list a fixed number of days that applies to every order.

How can I track my delivery?

Keep your order confirmation and check any shipping updates. Tracking information, when available, is provided after the parcel is handed to the carrier.

Returns and Refunds

How long do I have to return an item?

Lopop allows returns within 30 days. For terms and instructions, visit the page 30-day returns.

When are returns free?

The site advertises free returns on orders over €29.90. Always check the applicable terms before proceeding.

When will I receive my refund?

The site does not currently specify refund processing times, so no exact timeframe is given.
