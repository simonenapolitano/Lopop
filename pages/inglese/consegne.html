Shipping and Delivery

Find out more about delivery for your Lopop purchases.

Standard Delivery

Lopop offers standard delivery. The site advertises a €29.90 threshold for its shipping and returns promotion. Check your cart summary for the terms that apply to your order.

Delivery Times

Delivery times may depend on the destination and item availability. No specific delivery timeframe is listed; refer to the information shown at checkout before confirming your purchase.

Shipping Costs

Check any delivery charges in the order summary before payment so you can review the total before confirming your purchase.

Address and Receiving Your Order

When entering delivery details, make sure the address is complete and correct, including the street number, postcode, city and any useful information for the carrier.

If you cannot be there at delivery, follow any instructions left by the carrier to arrange redelivery or parcel collection.

For information about returning an item, visit the page 30-day returns. For other questions, visit the FAQ.
